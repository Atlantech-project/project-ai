## Findings on 12 random instances (8 requests, 2 berths, 4 tugs, 2 pilots)
- Every solve was OPTIMAL; CP-SAT placed at least as many requests as FIFO in all 12, and more in one (seed 2: 6 against 5).
- No conflict and no B2 violation in either plan.
- CP-SAT does not always wait less: seeds 2, 4 and 11 show more raw waiting minutes than FIFO. The DEC-23 objective weighs per-request normalised waiting against the tug resource cost with equal weights (CT-18 defaults), so it can take a later start for fewer tugs. This is the defined objective, not a defect. It is a point for the AI Team when the weights are tuned (CT-18 `objective_weight_*`).
